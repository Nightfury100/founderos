## Product Overview
**One-liner:** The brain (Atlas 5.1 AI) and infrastructure (AOS) for the $12T travel industry — a super-app for travelers and an intelligence layer for events and governments.
**Tagline:** Events. Travel. Cities. Powered by AI.
**What it does:** Atlas plans, prices and books an entire trip — flights, hotels, visa, eSIM, attractions, events — in under 10 seconds, matched to budget and preferences. For events, Tripy matches attendees with the right people, sessions and brands before day one, with venue navigation and gamified passes. AOS automates ~75% of travel operations.
**Product category:** AI travel planner / travel super-app (B2C); event intelligence platform (B2B); destination intelligence (B2G).
**Product type:** Consumer app + B2B/B2G platform.
**Business model:** Events intelligence B2B ($200K avg per mid-size event, 60–70% margin) → traveler booking & subscription B2C ($1,600 avg package) → social sponsors & brand deals ($5K–$50K) → in-search advertising ($20K–$100K) → B2G intelligence ($1M–$30M/yr).
**HQ / launch market:** Dubai. MVP launch Oct 2026 (UAE) → GCC expansion Apr 2027.

## Target Audience
**B2C:** Business travelers to Dubai/GCC (25M+ business travelers at GCC events yearly; GCC tourists spend $1,900 avg vs $1,200 global). Core unit: 4-day Dubai business trip, $1,568 AOV per couple.
**B2B:** Event organizers (summits, festivals, conferences) — e.g. WTS, TheBlock, AI Lisbon, SBC Summit.
**B2G:** Tourism authorities / governments (e.g. Maldives).
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Plan and book my whole trip without visiting 277 pages."
- "Help me meet the right people at this event before I arrive."
- "Give organizers / destinations real intelligence on visitors and drive local spend."

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Travelers visit 277 web pages before booking one trip; only 15% of organizers rate event networking "very effective"; the ecosystem is fragmented and costly (Booking Holdings spent $18B+, 67% of revenue, on opex in 2025).
**Why alternatives fall short:** Legacy platforms can't go AI-native; AI startups can't go end-to-end (plan + book + visa + events).

## Differentiation
- Atlas 5.1: chat & voice AI built specifically for travel, events and city intelligence.
- End-to-end: plan → price → book (incl. visa, eSIM, events) in <10 seconds.
- Event intelligence (attendee matching, navigation, gamified passes) — no AI trip planner above does this.
- GCC supplier partnerships and exclusive inventory agreements.
- Founder credibility: ex-Emirates Head of Product & Innovation (+240% booking conversion, +315% AOV), Najm Award, ex Amazon/Noon/Binance/Tabby, Forbes MENA 30U30.
